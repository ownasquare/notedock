# Monetization Hypothesis

This document is a hypothesis, not market, buyer, payment, revenue, usage, or demand proof.

## Free local value

The open-source app should remain genuinely useful for one editor: paste feedback, normalize it,
and export a checklist without an account or provider.

## Possible hosted value

A later evidence-gated team service could add:

- shared review rounds with one authoritative lock;
- role-based resolution of same-moment or contradictory notes; and
- reusable source adapters for approved exports.

## Buyer and pricing hypothesis

Possible buyer: a post-production lead or producer managing two to ten editors.
Possible boundary: charge for shared workspaces and history, never for local parsing/export.
Possible pricing test: `US$12–24` per active workspace per month.

No pricing page, checkout, entitlement, payment provider, analytics, or outreach exists. Before any
hosted build, a separately authorized demand process would need qualified interviews, observed
workaround cost, repeat-use evidence, and valid payment pull.
